# Strategy — One Word, One Username, One Reward

## The game

Judge-scored text, pairwise: a username judge compares two handles
on memorability, distinctiveness, and whether the name lands in one
word. The winning username is put on show on the bounty page as the
field's best handle.

## The entry (v1)

```
joni-gunawan
```

12 characters, one word.

## Why this recipe

- **Real handle (authenticity):** this is Joni's actual handle across
  X, YouTube, Instagram and Threads (@ci3hu1 is the display name;
  joni-gunawan is the person). A real name carries a story a made-up
  handle cannot fake.
- **Two-part rhythm:** given name + family name, hyphen-joined — it
  reads as one word but carries two beats, easy to say, easy to
  remember, hard to confuse with a random string.
- **Distinctive:** "gunawan" is an Indonesian family name; in a field
  of generic gamer tags it stands out as a person's name, not a
  generated one.
- **Originality:** our own handle, not a copy of any entry already
  in — the field is judged pairwise, so a distinct voice matters more
  than matching a house style.

## Tried and dropped

- A invented "clever" handle (puns, leet-speak): reads as a generated
  tag and loses the authenticity the judge rewards.
- Shortening to "joni" alone: loses the second beat and collides with
  every other Joni in the field.

## Expected outcome

A valid gate (1–30 chars, UTF-8) and a competitive dev rate against
the house anchors. Blind rounds mean no live score until round 1 closes
(18:48 WIB); iterate from the round-1 board, keeping whatever the judge
rewards and fixing only what it penalises.
